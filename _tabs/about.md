I'm Vinh (aka **koga**), an Information Security undergraduate at the University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH).

This blog is a collection of CTF write-ups and notes from challenges I solve. Each write-up follows the process: the clues, the failed attempts, the tools, and the final solution.

Expect commands, code, and the occasional "why did that work?" moment. Finding the flag is satisfying; understanding how I got there is the point.
